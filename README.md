# Pravaah OS

> **Purpose:** The living operating system of Pravaah Dynamics.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-07  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [The Pravaah Handbook](handbook/README.md), [Changelog](changelog/CHANGELOG.md), [Product 001](website/README.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 4 minutes

Pravaah OS is how Pravaah Dynamics preserves clarity as it grows. It contains the principles, playbooks, templates, decisions, and product specifications that help a small team work with the care of a mature company.

Pravaah means flow. Thoughtful design, clear strategy, and dependable technology create trust. Trust creates the momentum that moves a business forward.

## Start here

1. Read [The Pravaah Way](handbook/the-pravaah-way.md).
2. Read [How to use this handbook](handbook/how-to-use-this-handbook.md).
3. Read the [company principles](company/principles/README.md).
4. Use the relevant playbook before beginning client, design, engineering, or product work.

## What lives here

- `handbook/` — the approachable internal manual for every team member.
- `company/` — identity, principles, culture, and leadership.
- `design/`, `engineering/` — standards for making durable work.
- `operations/`, `sales/`, `marketing/`, `legal/` — how the company runs.
- `templates/` — reusable working documents.
- `website/` — Product 001: Pravaah.com.
- `decisions/` — architecture decision records and rejected ideas.
- `knowledge/` — curated, attributable learning.

## Versioning

Pravaah OS uses semantic versioning. A patch release corrects or clarifies existing guidance. A minor release adds compatible guidance or playbooks. A major release changes a principle, operating model, or standard in a way that needs deliberate re-adoption. Record every release in the [changelog](changelog/CHANGELOG.md) and significant decisions in an [ADR](decisions/README.md).

## Current status

This is a pre-launch company operating system. Product 001 is specified here but has not been implemented. No claims of clients, results, awards, or published work should be made until they are true and approved.
