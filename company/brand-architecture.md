## Decision

The public brand is **Pravaah**. The internal company name is **Pravaah Dynamics**.

## Use Pravaah externally

Use **Pravaah** for the website, logo, public product names, public social profiles, proposals, presentations, editorial publishing, and everyday client communication. This keeps the identity concise, memorable, and focused on the experience people encounter.

## Use Pravaah Dynamics internally and legally

Use **Pravaah Dynamics** in Pravaah OS, internal records, legal review drafts, company administration, and, once verified, legal agreements, invoices, tax material, and regulatory disclosures. Final legal naming remains subject to incorporation and counsel review.

## Why this distinction exists

A company can have a clear public face without losing administrative precision. The brand should feel simple to a client; the operating system must remain exact about ownership and responsibility.

## Naming rule

Do not alternate names casually in the same public asset. Use `Pravaah` unless a legal or formal company reference is necessary. When both are needed, write `Pravaah, operated by Pravaah Dynamics` only after legal review confirms the wording.
