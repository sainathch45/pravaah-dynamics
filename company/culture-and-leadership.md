## Culture

Pravaah values calm responsibility. People should have enough context to exercise judgment, enough safety to ask questions, and enough ownership to improve what they touch. We prefer useful candor to performance and steady quality to visible busyness.

## Leadership

Leaders create clarity, remove avoidable friction, and give credit away. They explain trade-offs, make decisions when needed, and accept accountability for outcomes. Authority exists to serve the work and the people doing it.

## Decision rule

Choose the option that best protects trust, solves the real problem, and will still make sense when the company is larger. Record consequential choices in an ADR.
