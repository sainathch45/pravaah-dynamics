# Future Vision

> **Purpose:** Keep the brand broad enough for Pravaah’s future without pretending that future already exists.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-07  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Product 001](../website/README.md), [Engineering Philosophy](principles/engineering-philosophy.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 2 minutes

Pravaah may eventually create business software, AI products, automation tools, internal platforms, SaaS products, education, and research. This is a direction, not a claim about current offerings.

The company should grow by carrying forward its standards: useful technology, considered design, and a clear reason for every feature. Future products are numbered and documented as products so they receive the same care as client work.
