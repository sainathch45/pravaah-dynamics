# Hiring Philosophy

> **Purpose:** Set the standard for adding people without diluting Pravaah’s care.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-07  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Culture and Leadership](culture-and-leadership.md), [The Pravaah Handbook](../handbook/README.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

Hire people who are curious, dependable, and generous with their judgment. Skill matters, but Pravaah also needs people who can explain their thinking, learn in public, and improve a system instead of working around it.

Assess candidates through realistic work, clear conversations, and respect for their time. Do not hire for sameness. Hire for complementary strengths held together by shared standards.

Every new team member begins with the Handbook because onboarding is not administration; it is how the company makes its values usable.
