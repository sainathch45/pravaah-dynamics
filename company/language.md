## Voice

Write with calm confidence. Be direct, specific, and human. Prefer short sentences and ordinary words. Explain the reason before the mechanism when the reader needs context.

## Words we use

Momentum, craftsmanship, thoughtful, purposeful, digital experiences, systems, clarity, growth, trust, dependable, and considered.

Use these only when the work earns them. They are signals of intent, not decoration.

## Words we avoid

Cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, best-in-class, revolutionary, disruptive, leverage, unlock, empower, AI-powered, game-changing, and seamless solutions.

These phrases make broad claims without evidence. Replace them with the actual problem, action, and result.

## Style rules

- Use active voice when it makes responsibility clear.
- Do not invent numbers, clients, testimonials, awards, or history.
- Do not use hype to cover uncertainty.
- Write “AI integration” only when describing a specific capability, not as a label of prestige.

## Examples

| Context | Prefer | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website | “Build a clearer digital foundation.” | “Unlock your digital potential.” |
| Sales | “We will first understand the bottleneck.” | “We leverage proven solutions.” |
| Product | “This workflow reduces repeated manual steps.” | “An AI-powered transformation.” |
| Internal | “The decision needs evidence from users.” | “This is obviously best.” |
