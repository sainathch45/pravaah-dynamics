# Client Relationships

> **Purpose:** Explain how Pravaah earns and preserves client trust.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-07  
> **Owner:** Growth Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Client Delivery](../../operations/README.md), [Sales Principles](../../sales/README.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 2 minutes

## Why

The work succeeds when a client can make better business decisions, not simply when a deliverable is handed over.

## In practice

Be candid about scope, risk, and trade-offs. Learn the business before recommending work. Make progress visible. Protect the client from unnecessary complexity, including complexity that benefits us.

## We reject

Overselling, surprises, dependency by obscurity, and work that looks finished but is not ready to use.
