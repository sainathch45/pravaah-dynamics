# Communication

> **Purpose:** Set the standard for communication that creates confidence.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-07  
> **Owner:** Growth Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Pravaah Language](../language.md), [Weekly Communication](../../operations/weekly-communication.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 2 minutes

## Why

People trust what they can understand. Clear communication protects relationships, decisions, and time.

## In practice

Lead with the point. Name uncertainty. Use the right amount of detail for the reader. End with an owner and next step when action is required.

## We reject

Jargon, hidden bad news, performative complexity, and silence where an update is owed.
