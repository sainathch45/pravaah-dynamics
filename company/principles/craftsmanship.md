## Why

Craftsmanship earns trust because people notice when details support their needs rather than the maker’s ego.

## In practice

Understand the job before choosing a solution. Finish the small details that affect comprehension, reliability, and handover. Leave the work easier to maintain than you found it.

## We reject

Polish without purpose, rushed handoffs, and complexity that only displays technical ability.
