# Decision-making

> **Purpose:** Make significant choices visible, reasoned, and reversible where possible.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-07  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [ADR Guide](../../decisions/README.md), [Thinking](thinking.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

## Why

Undocumented decisions are easily relitigated and poorly inherited. Good records create institutional memory without slowing ordinary work.

## In practice

Decide close to the work when the impact is contained. Escalate choices that affect brand, clients, security, money, architecture, or other teams. Record consequential decisions using an ADR.

## We reject

Consensus theater, decisions by loudness, and permanent choices made without examining alternatives.
