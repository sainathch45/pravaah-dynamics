# Design Philosophy

> **Purpose:** Guide design toward comprehension, confidence, and lasting quality.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-07  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Design Principles](../../design/principles.md), [Rejected Ideas](../../decisions/rejected-ideas.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 2 minutes

## Why

Design earns trust by making a business easier to understand and use. Attention is borrowed from people; it should never be wasted.

## In practice

Create hierarchy before decoration. Use space, type, movement, and imagery to guide rather than distract. Build systems that support a unique expression without sacrificing coherence.

## We reject

Trend-chasing, decorative motion, copied layouts, and visual noise that makes the user work harder.
