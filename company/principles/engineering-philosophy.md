# Engineering Philosophy

> **Purpose:** Ground technical choices in reliability and maintainability.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-07  
> **Owner:** Technology Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Engineering Standards](../../engineering/standards.md), [Architecture](../../engineering/architecture.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 2 minutes

## Why

Technology creates trust when it works quietly, remains understandable, and can be safely changed.

## In practice

Choose the smallest dependable system that meets the real need. Make failure understandable. Prefer readable code, explicit boundaries, and measured performance to novelty.

## We reject

Tooling for its own sake, opaque systems, untested critical paths, and technical shortcuts hidden from the client or future team.
