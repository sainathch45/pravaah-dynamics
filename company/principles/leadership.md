# Leadership

> **Purpose:** Define leadership as a service to people and work.

> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-07  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Culture and Leadership](../culture-and-leadership.md), [Working Together](../../handbook/working-together.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 2 minutes

## Why

Teams do their best work when expectations are clear and responsibility is shared fairly.

## In practice

Give context, make decisions, remove friction, develop judgment, and own outcomes. Credit the team; take responsibility for the gaps.

## We reject

Control without context, blame, ambiguity used as authority, and leaders who ask others to carry risks they will not own.
