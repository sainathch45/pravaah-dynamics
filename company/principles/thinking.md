## Why

Busy work can look like progress while moving the business nowhere. Thought clarifies the problem before effort hardens into the wrong answer.

## In practice

Ask what is true, what matters, and what evidence is missing. State assumptions. Separate a symptom from its cause. Prefer a clear question to a confident guess.

## We reject

Premature certainty, borrowed solutions, and activity performed only because it is familiar.
