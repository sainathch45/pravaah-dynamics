> **Purpose:** State the long horizon and present responsibility of Pravaah Dynamics.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0

## Vision

To become a respected design and technology company known for creating enduring momentum for businesses in India and beyond.

## Mission

Help businesses build trust and move forward through thoughtful design, clear strategy, and dependable technology.

## Why this matters

The vision keeps Pravaah from optimizing only for the next project. The mission keeps that ambition practical: every engagement should improve a real business situation, not merely produce an attractive artifact.
