# ADR-001: Pravaah OS as a Living Handbook

> **Purpose:** Record why the company system is centred on a handbook rather than isolated documentation.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [The Pravaah Handbook](../handbook/README.md), [Changelog](../changelog/CHANGELOG.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

## Context

Pravaah needs standards that can guide future employees, not only a one-time website brief.

## Decision

Treat the repository as Pravaah OS and make `/handbook` the approachable entry point to the company’s living manual.

## Alternatives considered

- Keep a loose collection of functional documentation.
- Maintain only project-specific documents.

## Reasoning

A handbook makes the system readable and useful in daily work. It gives every discipline a shared home while preserving the reasons behind standards.

## Consequences

Every material process needs ownership, a review path, and a consistent document header. Changes to core principles require ADRs and changelog entries.

## Status and review

Accepted. Review by 2027-08-08 or when the first non-founder employee joins.
