# ADR-002: Positioning and Engagement Model

> **Purpose:** Record the initial market focus and how Pravaah describes its work.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Company](../company/README.md), [Sales](../sales/README.md), [Product 001](../website/README.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

## Context

Pravaah is pre-launch and needs a focused but expandable first public position.

## Decision

Serve growing, service-led businesses in India, while remaining globally readable. Frame the offer as problem-solving through Foundations, Experiences, and Systems rather than a conventional list of services.

## Alternatives considered

- Lead only with product engineering.
- Present a broad, sector-neutral agency offer.
- Package fixed service tiers as the primary message.

## Reasoning

The chosen model fits the founders’ complementary strengths and speaks to a business need without making Pravaah feel limited to websites or dependent on a portfolio it does not yet have.

## Consequences

Sales, website, and marketing content must explain outcomes and working style. New markets or engagement types require evidence and a positioning review.

## Status and review

Accepted. Review after the first five qualified opportunities or by 2027-02-08.
