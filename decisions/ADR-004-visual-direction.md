# ADR-004: Editorial-Architectural Visual Direction

> **Purpose:** Record the visual character that should guide Product 001 and future Pravaah expressions.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Design System](../design/design-system.md), [Rejected Ideas](rejected-ideas.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

## Context

Pravaah needs a premium identity that is calm, timeless, and not visually limited to current technology trends.

## Decision

Use an editorial-architectural direction: restrained typography, generous whitespace, neutral tones, precise composition, and purposeful motion.

## Alternatives considered

- A warm, tactile craft-led identity.
- A more technical, systems-forward visual expression.
- A trend-led startup aesthetic.

## Reasoning

The direction supports thoughtfulness and trust without becoming cold or generic. It creates room for future products and client work to have their own character within shared principles.

## Consequences

Design decisions must be evaluated for hierarchy, function, and longevity. Trend-driven visual patterns remain rejected unless the context and evidence have materially changed.

## Status and review

Accepted. Review after Product 001 design direction is validated with real content.
