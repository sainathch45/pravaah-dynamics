# ADR-005: Truth-First Public Launch

> **Purpose:** Record the boundary against invented proof and premature marketing claims.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Growth Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Product 001](../website/README.md), [Case Study Framework](../marketing/case-studies.md), [Pravaah Language](../company/language.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

## Context

Pravaah is pre-launch and has no approved portfolio, testimonials, awards, or Journal material.

## Decision

Launch Product 001 with principles, approach, engagements, and conversation flow only. Do not create fictional proof, concept work presented as client work, or filler editorial content.

## Alternatives considered

- Publish self-initiated concept work.
- Delay the website until client work exists.
- Use generic social proof patterns without naming clients.

## Reasoning

Trust is foundational to Pravaah. Honest incompleteness is stronger than a polished claim that cannot be substantiated.

## Consequences

Future case studies, testimonials, and Journal entries require real source material and appropriate approval. The initial site must earn attention through clarity and craft.

## Status and review

Accepted. Review when the first client project reaches a publishable, approved outcome.
