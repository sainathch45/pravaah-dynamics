# ADR-007: Public Brand and Visual Identity Direction

> **Purpose:** Record Pravaah’s public naming convention and selected visual-identity direction.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Brand Architecture](../company/brand-architecture.md), [Visual Identity](../design/identity/README.md), [Logo Decision Matrix](../design/identity/decision-matrix.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 4 minutes

## Context

Product 001 and future communications need one concise public identity. The company also needs a clear internal and legal name for operating records and eventual formal use.

## Decision

Use **Pravaah** as the external brand, including website, logo, public profiles, presentations, proposals, and product identity. Use **Pravaah Dynamics** for internal company records and legal contexts once incorporation and counsel review establish the required wording.

Select **The Measured Wordmark** as the visual-identity direction: a typography-led wordmark, charcoal and paper foundation, restrained moss and copper accents, and a fine flow line as a supporting device. Launch without a standalone brand icon; keep the Flow Monogram in future exploration only.

## Alternatives considered

- Use Pravaah Dynamics externally everywhere.
- Create a literal flowing `P` or water-based symbol now.
- Use a generic abstract technology or AI mark.
- Create final visual assets before the identity system and applications were defined.

## Reasoning

Pravaah is clearer and more memorable in public. A typography-led identity is appropriate for a company whose distinctiveness must be earned through work, not implied by a decorative icon. Defining the system before artwork protects consistency and lowers future implementation churn.

## Consequences

Public Product 001 copy and metadata use Pravaah. Legal and internal documents retain Pravaah Dynamics where precision requires it. Final wordmark, compact mark, favicon, social assets, and physical applications require founder approval before production.

## Status and review

Accepted direction pending founder approval of final artwork. Review after the first visual-identity presentation and before any production asset is released.
