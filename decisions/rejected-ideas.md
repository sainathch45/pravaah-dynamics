# Rejected Ideas

> **Purpose:** Preserve deliberately avoided directions so future work does not reintroduce them by accident.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Design Principles](../design/principles.md), [ADR-004](ADR-004-visual-direction.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

Rejected ideas are not permanent prohibitions. They are decisions made in context. Reconsider one only when the use case, evidence, and brand fit change enough to justify a new ADR.

| Idea | Why rejected | Review condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Glassmorphism | It often reduces contrast and makes structure feel decorative rather than dependable. | A specific accessibility-tested product need. |
| Purple gradients | They are strongly associated with generic startup aesthetics and do not support the chosen visual direction. | A future brand system with a documented strategic reason. |
| Neon and cyberpunk styling | It makes technology the spectacle when Pravaah wants technology to feel quiet. | A clearly distinct product audience and approved product identity. |
| Glowing-card layouts | They turn content into repeated visual objects and weaken editorial hierarchy. | A functional state or data visualization that needs emphasis. |
| Robot illustrations | They flatten complex technology into a generic visual shorthand. | A specific educational context where a better metaphor is unavailable. |
| Stock photography by default | It creates a borrowed, undifferentiated story and can misrepresent a company. | A rights-cleared image with a precise editorial purpose. |
| Template-led agency pages | They organise the site around convention rather than the visitor’s real questions. | Evidence that a conventional pattern improves comprehension for a defined audience. |
