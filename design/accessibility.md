# Accessibility

> **Purpose:** Ensure Pravaah design is usable by more people in more situations.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Technology Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Design System](design-system.md), [Engineering Standards](../engineering/standards.md), [Website Accessibility](../website/accessibility.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 4 minutes

Accessibility is a trust practice. A polished interface that excludes people is not high quality.

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA as the working baseline for public products.
- Maintain sufficient colour contrast; never rely on colour alone for meaning.
- Design visible focus states, clear error messages, logical reading order, and usable keyboard paths.
- Use real content and long translations when reviewing layouts.
- Honour reduced-motion preferences and support responsive zoom.

Accessibility review begins during design, not after implementation. Treat a barrier as a design problem to solve, not a compliance defect to hide.
