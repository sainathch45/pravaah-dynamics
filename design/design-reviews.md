# Design Reviews

> **Purpose:** Make design critique specific, constructive, and tied to outcomes.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Design Principles](principles.md), [Design Review Template](../templates/design-review.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

## Before the review

State the problem, intended audience, constraints, and decision needed. Share the work early enough that feedback can still improve it.

## During the review

Discuss the hierarchy, comprehension, business fit, content, accessibility, and implementation implications before personal preference. Ask what evidence supports a comment.

## After the review

Record decisions, open questions, owner, and next review point. A review is useful when it changes the work or strengthens confidence in leaving it unchanged.
