## Digital profiles

| Application | Required expression | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn company page | Paper or charcoal field, wordmark, single flow-line composition, concise brand statement. | Generic cityscape, stock people, marketing collage. |
| GitHub organisation | Compact wordmark or approved future monogram; readable organisation description. | A decorative icon that is unclear at small size. |
| Open Graph / social card | Wordmark, route title, paper or charcoal field, one restrained line; text-first layout. | Screenshots, client logos, fake work, dense illustration. |
| Favicon and app icon | Future approved compact mark, monochrome, tested at 16px. | Tiny wordmark, detailed linework, gradients. |
| Presentation cover | Large wordmark or title, clear hierarchy, paper/charcoal field, measured line. | Template cover with many decorative shapes. |

## Business documents

**Proposal cover:** client name and project intent carry the hierarchy; Pravaah wordmark is quiet but present. Use paper field, charcoal text, a small moss or copper line, and plenty of margin.

**Invoice:** clarity leads. The wordmark, legal company name where required, invoice facts, and payment details must outrank visual expression. Use no decorative image or texture behind tabular data.

**Letterhead and email signature:** make the sender, role, contact path, and company name easy to find. An email signature is not a banner; use the wordmark or text name once, no quote, no icon row until official accounts exist.

## Physical applications

**Office signage:** charcoal or paper wordmark with clear space, material honesty, and no illuminated gimmick. Use the building and context to determine scale.

**Laptop sticker:** future compact mark or wordmark only, one colour, generous edge space. It should look intentional from a distance and remain legible up close.

**Notebook:** wordmark or small flow line at low emphasis; inside pages prioritise writing space.

**Business card:** name and role on one side; Pravaah wordmark and contact method on the other. Use weight, space, and quality stock rather than visual clutter.

**Coffee mug or packaging:** one-colour wordmark or compact mark. The object is not a brand billboard; the application should feel considered and useful.

## Adaptation rule

Every application uses the same typographic hierarchy, material restraint, colour roles, and flow-line logic. It may change composition to suit its medium. Consistency of judgment matters more than repeating the same layout.
