# Colour Psychology and Palette

> **Purpose:** Define Pravaah’s colour roles through intended experience, not vague claims about universal psychology.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Visual Design](../../website/design/visual-design.md), [Wordmark System](wordmark-system.md), [Texture Language](texture-language.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 7 minutes

Colour does not create trust by itself. Its meaning comes from consistency, contrast, material context, and the quality of what surrounds it. Pravaah’s palette is intentionally restrained so colour can signal purpose rather than compete for attention.

| Colour | Value | Role | Intended feeling | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Charcoal | `#111312` | Foundation and primary ink | Grounded, quiet, dependable | Default dark surface and primary text. |
| Paper | `#F7F6F2` | Primary light surface | Spacious, considered, tactile | Default reading surface; never pure white by default. |
| Moss | `#355449` | Growth and direction | Steady, organic, measured | Functional accent, links, focus, and selective emphasis. |
| Copper | `#A5603A` | Human energy and occasion | Warmth, movement, craft | Rare accent for print, editorial markers, and selected moments; never an error or primary CTA colour. |
| Stone | `#B9B6AE` | Material support | Balance, restraint | Rules, secondary surfaces, and quiet structural detail. |

## Why charcoal

Charcoal has more softness than pure black and more authority than a fashionable near-black. It makes paper, typography, and real materials feel deliberate. It is the colour of foundation, not drama.

## Why paper

Paper makes digital reading feel less clinical and gives the editorial system physical warmth. It reinforces that Pravaah is considered without pretending the brand is handmade or nostalgic.

## Why moss

Moss suggests sustained growth rather than instant growth. It is the colour of a system that is alive and maintained. Because it is used sparingly, a link or focus state feels intentional instead of decorative.

## Why copper

Copper introduces warmth and movement without neon energy. It belongs to moments of emphasis, material print, and human touch. Overuse would make Pravaah feel ornamental, so its maximum screen-area use is 5% on any single view.

## Accessibility and combinations

Charcoal on paper and paper on charcoal are core text combinations. Moss is validated for text and focus use against paper before release. Copper is not used for small text or critical controls unless tested to the applicable contrast requirement. Never convey state using colour alone.

## Application balance

Default composition: 80–90% paper or charcoal, 8–15% stone or moss support, 0–5% copper. This is a composition guide, not a mechanical ratio. A one-colour application is preferable to a busy multicolour one.
