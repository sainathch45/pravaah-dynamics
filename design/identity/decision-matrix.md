## Recommended choice

Select **The Measured Wordmark** as the Pravaah primary identity. It places the name at the centre, works where the company actually needs to communicate, and supports the chosen editorial-architectural direction without borrowing a fashionable startup visual language.

Pair it with **The Horizon Line** as a supporting compositional device, not as a second logo. Keep **The Flow Monogram** in exploration only until it proves legible, memorable, and unlike a generic technology symbol.

## Rejected for v1

Reject a literal flowing `P`, an infinity loop, a water droplet, a wave mark, a circuit-board symbol, a gradient mark, an abstract AI glyph, a chrome or glowing mark, and a decorative seal. Each would make the idea of flow too literal, too fashionable, or too disconnected from the business clarity Pravaah needs.

## Approval gate

The founders approve the direction before any final logo artwork is produced. Artwork review must include monochrome, small-size, contrast, print, digital, legal-name, and application tests. If a finalist fails those tests, revisit the exploration rather than forcing it into production.
