## Brand-icon decision

Pravaah launches with no standalone brand icon. The wordmark is the recognisable signature; the flow line is a compositional device, not a substitute for the name. This is the most honest choice for a new brand: recognition should be earned before a symbol is asked to carry it.

Direction 02, the Flow Monogram, may be explored in Layer 3 as a future compact identifier. It advances only if it remains legible at 16px, feels distinct without explanation, and does not turn the capital `P` into a generic technology glyph. It does not ship at launch by default.

## Functional icon library

- Base grid: 24 by 24px; provide 16px and 20px optical variants only when clarity needs them.
- Stroke: 1.5px, round cap, round join; 2px only for icons that lose clarity at 16px.
- Perspective: orthographic and front-facing. No 3D perspective or simulated material.
- Geometry: simple open forms, generous internal space, and a maximum 2px corner radius for closed shapes.
- Fill: outlined by default. A filled form is reserved for active or selected state, paired with a textual or structural state signal.
- Colour: current text colour. Icons do not introduce a separate decorative palette.
- Labels: icons that are not universally understood always include visible text. Tooltips supplement but do not replace labels.

## Core launch icons

Only include navigation menu, close, arrow forward, arrow back where needed, external link, and success/error symbols if they clarify a state. Do not create pictograms for Foundations, Experiences, or Systems; language explains those concepts more honestly.
