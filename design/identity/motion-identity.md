# Motion Identity

> **Purpose:** Define how Pravaah should feel in motion across products and communications.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Technology Lead

> **Estimated Reading Time:** 6 minutes

Pravaah motion feels like a measured current, not water as spectacle. It has direction, continuity, and enough resistance to feel real. It should suggest a calm breath or ink finding a path across paper: one deliberate movement, then stillness.

## Motion qualities

- **Directed:** movement has a clear origin and destination.
- **Continuous:** elements do not pop, bounce, or overshoot without a functional reason.
- **Calm:** acceleration is gentle; the resting state arrives with confidence.
- **Brief:** motion reveals a relationship, then gets out of the way.
- **Human:** pacing leaves enough time to perceive change without making the user wait.

## Signature gesture

The flow line may extend, connect, or settle. It never loops indefinitely, ripples like water, behaves like smoke, or chases a cursor. It is a quiet gesture of progress, not a logo animation.

## Identity rules across media

On a website, motion supports navigation and hierarchy. In a presentation, it introduces one idea at a time. In social media, it may reveal a thought or structure over a short, readable sequence. In print, motion becomes implied direction through line, spacing, and page order.

Reduced motion always replaces movement with an immediate stable state. The identity remains recognisable through composition, type, and colour alone.
