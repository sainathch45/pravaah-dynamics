# Texture Language

> **Purpose:** Define the restrained material cues that keep Pravaah from feeling flat without becoming decorative.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Colour Psychology](color-psychology.md), [Illustration Direction](illustration.md), [Photography Direction](photography.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 5 minutes

Texture is an environmental cue, not an effect. It should make an experience feel considered and materially grounded while remaining almost invisible to a person trying to read or act.

## Approved materials

**Paper:** the primary digital texture. A near-imperceptible, static grain may support large paper fields in print, presentation, or selected editorial screens.

**Stone and concrete:** photographic or print materials that express permanence and structure. They are never imitated through noisy UI backgrounds.

**Ink:** expressed through the charcoal palette, typography, and the fine flow line. It suggests precision rather than calligraphy.

**Light:** expressed through natural photography and open paper space, never by glows, flares, or moving rays.

## Prohibited treatments

Glassmorphism, frosted panels, heavy blur, neon, glossy 3D forms, gradient meshes, animated noise, liquid blobs, chrome, ornamental water effects, and visible computer-generated grain. These compete with content and would make the brand feel trend-led.

## Technical and accessibility guardrails

Digital grain must be static, optional, below 20KB compressed, and disabled where it risks contrast or performance. It may not sit beneath body text. Texture never becomes a loading state, hover effect, or substitute for visual hierarchy.
