## Primary wordmark

The final wordmark reads `Pravaah` with an initial capital and lowercase remainder. It is a custom-drawn refinement of an editorial serif construction, not a default font setting. Its character should feel composed, warm, and precise rather than calligraphic, ornate, or aggressively geometric.

## Optical construction

- Correct letter spacing by eye, not by uniform mathematical tracking. The `r-a`, `a-v`, and final `a-h` relationships require individual review because visual space differs from measured space.
- The wordmark baseline is level. Do not tilt, warp, join letters with a literal wave, or make the `P` a separate illustration.
- The capital `P` has presence but does not dominate the word. The lowercase forms should carry the rhythm.
- The final artwork must preserve open counters and stable strokes at small sizes.

## Lockups and alignment

The wordmark is the primary lockup. The optional flow line sits below or beside it only in a specified application layout; it never intersects letters. No stacked symbol-plus-wordmark lockup is required at launch.

Align the wordmark to the same grid edge as headline text. Do not centre it by default. Centre alignment is reserved for formal covers or small-format applications where the composition needs it.

## Clear space and size

Clear space is at least one `a`-height on all sides of the final wordmark. No line, image edge, text, or container boundary enters that area. Minimum digital height is 24px for the primary wordmark and 18px only for approved compact contexts after legibility testing. There is no maximum size, but at large scale the wordmark retains generous clear space and is never used as a decorative wallpaper.

## Light and dark use

Use charcoal wordmark on paper surfaces and paper wordmark on charcoal surfaces. The moss or copper accent may never be the sole default wordmark colour; it is reserved for a deliberate, high-contrast application approved by the identity system. Do not add gradients, strokes, shadows, textures, opacity effects, or animation to the wordmark.

## Brand versus company name

Public wordmark: `Pravaah`. Legal or internal company references use `Pravaah Dynamics` as text, not as a second logo. This avoids two competing public identities.
