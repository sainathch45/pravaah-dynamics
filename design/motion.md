# Motion System

> **Purpose:** Make motion calm, useful, accessible, and consistent.

## Why motion exists

Motion can explain change, reveal hierarchy, and provide feedback. It should make an interaction feel more understandable, never more theatrical.

## Rules

- Animate only when a person benefits from seeing the relationship between states.
- Use short, calm transitions with natural deceleration; do not create spectacle from routine actions.
- Preserve the final state if animation is disabled or reduced.
- Never gate content, navigation, or essential feedback behind an animation.
- Test on reduced-motion preferences and constrained devices.

## We avoid

Parallax for decoration, endlessly moving backgrounds, attention-seeking reveals, and movement that makes a page harder to scan.
