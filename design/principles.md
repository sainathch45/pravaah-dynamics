# Design Principles

> **Purpose:** Provide durable reasons behind Pravaah design decisions.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Design System](design-system.md), [Rejected Ideas](../decisions/rejected-ideas.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 5 minutes

## Clarity before creativity

People cannot value what they cannot understand. Establish hierarchy, language, and intent before adding expression.

## Every pixel has purpose

Visual decisions should support comprehension, confidence, or a deliberate feeling. Remove elements that only fill space or signal fashion.

## Technology should disappear

Interfaces should help a person act without asking them to admire the system. Complexity belongs behind the experience when possible.

## Design earns trust

Consistency, accessible behavior, honest language, and careful detail show that a business will treat people well after they inquire.

## Motion should guide

Movement establishes relationships, confirms change, or directs attention. It must not delay reading, create surprise, or compete with content.

## Build for longevity

Use a flexible system rather than a fixed look. A durable system can adapt across future products and clients without becoming generic.

## Respect attention

Attention is limited. Make the important thing easy to find, then let the user choose their depth of engagement.

## Leave things simpler

Every pass should make the interface, language, or system easier for the next person to understand.
