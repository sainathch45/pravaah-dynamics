# Photography, Iconography, and Illustration

> **Purpose:** Set a consistent, rights-aware approach to visual assets.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Design System](design-system.md), [Rejected Ideas](../decisions/rejected-ideas.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

Use assets to clarify a real idea, establish credible context, or create deliberate atmosphere. Prefer original or commissioned material. When external assets are used, confirm rights, licence, attribution, and whether the image truthfully represents the business.

Photography should feel observed rather than staged. Iconography should be sparse, legible, and consistent in visual weight. Illustration should explain a concept that text or photography cannot explain more clearly. Do not use imagery as a substitute for a missing message.
