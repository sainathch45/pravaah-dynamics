Treat deployment as a product capability, not a final manual step. Use reproducible builds, protected environment configuration, preview environments where useful, and an explicit production verification checklist.

For Product 001, Vercel is the recommended hosting and deployment platform because it supports the proposed Next.js stack with straightforward previews, image optimization, and edge delivery. The final account, domain, monitoring, and analytics choices require founder approval at implementation time.
