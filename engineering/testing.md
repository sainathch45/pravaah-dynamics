# Testing and Quality

> **Purpose:** Define confidence-building checks appropriate to the risk of a change.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Technology Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Delivery](delivery.md), [Performance](performance.md), [Website Testing](../website/acceptance.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 4 minutes

Test behavior that would harm users, clients, or the business if it failed. Use unit tests for logic, integration tests for boundaries, and end-to-end checks for critical journeys. Pair automated checks with accessible manual review where visual or human judgment matters.

The right amount of testing follows risk. A small content correction does not need the same evidence as payment, authentication, data handling, or lead capture.
