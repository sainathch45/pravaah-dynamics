# Working Together

> **Purpose:** Set the daily behaviors that make a small team reliable and kind.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-07  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Communication Principle](../company/principles/communication.md), [Internal Meetings](../operations/internal-meetings.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 4 minutes

## Default to clarity

Write down decisions, owners, and next steps. Clarity is respectful because it saves others from reconstructing intent.

## Bring the problem with the proposal

Surface risks early, state the evidence, and offer a considered next move. Escalation is not failure; hidden ambiguity is more expensive.

## Protect focused work

Use meetings for decisions, working sessions, and relationships. Use written updates for status. This preserves time for the craft clients are paying for.

## Disagree with care

Challenge ideas, not people. State the trade-off and listen for context. Once a decision is made, support it unless new evidence changes the case.

## Close loops

Respond when you own an action. Tell people when a promise is complete, blocked, or changed. Reliability is created in small moments.
