> **Estimated Reading Time:** 4 minutes

Pravaah becomes more capable when learning survives the moment it was discovered. The knowledge base is a curated index, not a dumping ground for links.

## Capture rule

For every entry, record the source, creator, date accessed, category, a short summary in your own words, why it matters to Pravaah, possible application, and any rights or attribution requirement. Do not reproduce copyrighted material beyond what is needed for fair internal reference.

## Curation rule

Add only material that may influence a decision, practice, or point of view. Review entries quarterly; archive obsolete material rather than letting the index imply current endorsement.
