## Important notice

These are India-oriented pre-incorporation working templates, not legal advice and not final legal documents. They must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel and completed with the legal entity, address, contact, tax, jurisdiction, and data-processing details before client or public use.

The purpose of early legal documentation is to make risks visible now, not to simulate legal certainty.
