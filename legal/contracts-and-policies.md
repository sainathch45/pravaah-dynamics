**Pre-incorporation template. Legal review required before use.**

The required reviewed set may include a master services agreement or project contract, statement of work, proposal terms, NDA, invoice terms, privacy policy, cookie policy, website terms, and data-processing terms where applicable. The exact set depends on the entity, services, clients, data, payment process, and jurisdictions involved.

Do not copy generic web policies or contracts into production. Each document must reflect actual practices and be approved by qualified counsel.
