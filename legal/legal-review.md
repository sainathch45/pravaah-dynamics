# Legal Review Checklist

> **Purpose:** Define the information and professional review required before legal materials are used.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Legal Foundations](README.md), [Privacy and Cookies](../website/privacy-and-cookies.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

Before publishing or signing any policy, agreement, proposal term, contract, invoice term, or NDA, confirm the legal entity, registered and business addresses, authorised signatory, tax obligations, governing law, dispute process, insurance, intellectual-property ownership, data-processing roles, retention periods, and required consumer disclosures.

Engage qualified counsel in the applicable Indian jurisdiction. Where a client or service creates cross-border obligations, obtain advice for the relevant jurisdictions before relying on a template.
