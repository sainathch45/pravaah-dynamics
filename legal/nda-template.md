# NDA Review Template

> **Purpose:** Identify the terms a counsel-reviewed confidentiality agreement must contain.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Legal Review Checklist](legal-review.md), [Client Discovery](../operations/client-discovery.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 2 minutes

**Pre-incorporation template. Legal review required before use.**

A reviewed NDA should define confidential information, exclusions, permitted use, disclosure controls, security expectations, duration, return or destruction of information, remedies, governing law, and signing authority. Confirm whether a mutual NDA is appropriate before requesting one.
