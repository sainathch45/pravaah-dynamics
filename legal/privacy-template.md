# Privacy and Cookie Policy Inputs

> **Purpose:** Collect the facts required for a truthful public privacy and cookie policy.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Technology Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Website Privacy Requirements](../website/privacy-and-cookies.md), [Legal Review Checklist](legal-review.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

**Pre-incorporation template. Legal review required before use.**

Before publishing, document the entity and contact details, personal data collected, purpose and lawful basis, processors, analytics and cookies, retention, security controls, international transfers, user rights, complaint channel, and policy effective date.

The website must not publish a generic privacy policy that claims practices Pravaah has not verified.
