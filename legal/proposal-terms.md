**Pre-incorporation template. Legal review required before use.**

A final agreement should clearly cover scope, deliverables, client responsibilities, fees and taxes, payment timing, change control, timelines, feedback and approval, intellectual-property rights, confidentiality, third-party services, warranties and limitations, termination, support, governing law, and dispute handling.

Do not present this outline as binding terms. Counsel must turn it into an agreement appropriate to the engagement and entity.
