# Brand Voice

> **Purpose:** Apply Pravaah Language consistently across public communications.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Growth Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Pravaah Language](../company/language.md), [Copywriting Guide](../website/copywriting-guide.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

Pravaah sounds thoughtful, direct, calm, and specific. Write to help a business leader understand a choice, not to impress them with the vocabulary of an agency.

Make claims only when supported. Prefer a precise example to a broad promise. Avoid inflated adjectives, false urgency, and generic marketing phrases. The voice should make a reader feel that Pravaah has paid attention.
