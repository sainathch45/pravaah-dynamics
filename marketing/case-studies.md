# Case Study Framework

> **Purpose:** Capture real client work with accuracy and respect.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Growth Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Journal Framework](journal.md), [Client Relationships](../company/principles/client-relationships.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

Only publish a case study with client permission and verified facts. Explain the starting context, the problem, the approach, the work, the measurable or observed outcome, and the client’s role. Do not turn a collaborative effort into a hero story.

If outcomes are not yet known or approval is absent, do not publish the case study.
