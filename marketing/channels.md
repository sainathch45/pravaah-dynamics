# Marketing Channels and Photography

> **Purpose:** Apply Pravaah’s point of view consistently across LinkedIn, newsletters, social media, and visual publishing.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Growth Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Journal Framework](journal.md), [Brand Voice](brand-voice.md), [Visual Assets](../design/visual-assets.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 4 minutes

Use each channel for a real reader need. LinkedIn can share a clear observation, useful excerpt, or invitation to a relevant discussion. Newsletters should reward permission with considered work, not recycled promotion. Social formats should adapt a meaningful idea rather than reduce every thought to a trend.

Photography follows the visual-asset standard: original or rights-cleared, contextually honest, and purposeful. Maintain a source and permission record for every asset used publicly.
