> **Purpose:** Define the future editorial practice for the Pravaah Journal.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Growth Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Knowledge Base](../knowledge/README.md), [Case Study Framework](case-studies.md), [Product 001](../website/README.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 4 minutes

The Journal is not a conventional blog. It is where Pravaah shares useful ideas about design, engineering, business, AI, and the work of building trusted digital experiences.

## Formats

Essays, practical notes, project learnings, research observations, and approved case studies. Each piece needs a clear question, a named author or approved collective attribution, evidence or experience behind it, and an editorial review.

## Launch posture

The Journal launches with its framework and publishing capability only. Do not publish invented articles, case studies, or viewpoints. Founder-provided work may be added after editorial review.
