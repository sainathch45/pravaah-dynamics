Client operations make care visible. The goal is not to create more process; it is to make progress, responsibility, and decisions easy for the client to understand.

## Delivery path

1. Qualify the opportunity and discover the business problem.
2. Align on scope, outcomes, responsibilities, and agreement.
3. Kick off with shared context, working rhythm, and decision owners.
4. Design and build through visible reviews and weekly communication.
5. Verify, launch, hand over, and agree on the next support step.

Use the linked playbooks before each stage. Adapt the cadence to the client, but do not remove clarity about scope, risk, or ownership.
