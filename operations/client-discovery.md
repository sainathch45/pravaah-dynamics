## Why discovery comes first

The first request may describe a symptom, such as an outdated website or low conversion. Discovery protects both Pravaah and the client from investing in a polished answer to the wrong problem.

## Learn

- The business model, audience, offering, and current growth constraint.
- What success means and how the client currently observes it.
- Existing systems, content, owners, constraints, and decision process.
- Risks, dependencies, and what would make the work fail after launch.

## Onboarding outcome

Before work begins, provide a written summary of the problem, agreed outcome, scope boundaries, roles, working rhythm, risks, and next decision. If these cannot be made clear, pause rather than using a vague agreement as momentum.
