# Design, Development, and QA

> **Purpose:** Coordinate the making and verification of client work.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Technology Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Design Reviews](../design/design-reviews.md), [Engineering Delivery](../engineering/delivery.md), [QA Checklist](../templates/qa-checklist.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 5 minutes

Move from intent to detail in reviewable steps. Design reviews test whether the proposed experience solves the agreed problem. Development reviews test whether the system delivers that experience reliably. QA verifies the agreed acceptance criteria before launch.

Separate preferences from defects. Record approved changes and their effect on scope. Do not treat client approval as a substitute for accessibility, performance, security, or functional verification.
