# Invoices and Financial Administration

> **Purpose:** Keep commercial administration clear, timely, and ready for legal and tax review.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Invoice Template](../templates/invoice.md), [Pricing Framework](../sales/pricing.md), [Legal Review](../legal/legal-review.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

Invoice against an approved agreement or milestone. Keep proposals, approvals, invoices, receipts, and client communication organised together so the payment trail is understandable to the client and future finance support.

Before sending invoices, confirm legal entity, tax registration, required invoice fields, payment terms, and banking details with qualified advice. Financial clarity is part of client trust; it should not become a surprise at the end of good work.
