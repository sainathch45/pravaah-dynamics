# Internal Meetings and Knowledge Management

> **Purpose:** Use team time for alignment, decisions, and learning rather than status theatre.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Working Together](../handbook/working-together.md), [Knowledge Base](../knowledge/README.md), [Meeting Notes](../templates/meeting-notes.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

Meet only when conversation changes the quality or speed of a decision. Each meeting has a purpose, prepared context, facilitator, and written outcome. Status belongs in asynchronous updates unless it needs discussion.

Useful learning is captured in the knowledge base with a source, a short reason it matters, and a suggested application. Institutional memory should not depend on who happened to attend a call.
