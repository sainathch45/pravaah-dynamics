# Client Kickoff

> **Purpose:** Turn an agreement into a shared, workable start.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Growth Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Client Discovery](client-discovery.md), [Kickoff Template](../templates/kickoff.md), [Weekly Communication](weekly-communication.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

Kickoff aligns the people who will make the work succeed. Confirm the business problem, intended outcome, scope, timeline assumptions, communication channel, feedback process, approval owner, and access needed.

Send a written recap within one working day. It should name open questions and risks, not create the impression that uncertainty has disappeared.
