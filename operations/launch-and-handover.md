# Launch, Handover, and Support

> **Purpose:** Make the end of a project a reliable beginning for the client.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Technology Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Launch Checklist](../templates/launch-checklist.md), [Maintenance](maintenance.md), [Retrospective](../templates/retrospective.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 4 minutes

## Launch

Use the launch checklist to verify production behavior, domains, forms, analytics, performance, backups where applicable, and rollback ownership. Confirm client approval and communication plans before announcing anything.

## Handover

Hand over access, documentation, ownership, training, and known limitations. A client should understand what they own, what requires support, and how to obtain it.

## Retrospective

After launch, review the outcome, process, surprises, and reusable learning. Capture improvements in the handbook or an ADR so the next project benefits.
