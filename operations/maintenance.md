# Maintenance and Retainers

> **Purpose:** Define dependable support without creating vague open-ended commitments.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Growth Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Launch and Handover](launch-and-handover.md), [Pricing Framework](../sales/pricing.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

Support begins with a clear agreement about included work, response expectations, client responsibilities, access, and escalation. A retainer should buy an ongoing capacity or outcome, not an undefined promise to be available.

Review recurring work regularly. If the same issue repeats, propose a durable improvement rather than treating symptoms forever.
