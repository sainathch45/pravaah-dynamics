# Weekly Communication

> **Purpose:** Keep client confidence high through regular, useful visibility.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Growth Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Weekly Update Template](../templates/weekly-update.md), [Communication Principle](../company/principles/communication.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

Send an update on the agreed day each week, even when progress is blocked. Include what changed, what is next, decisions needed from the client, risks, and any effect on timing or scope.

Weekly updates exist because silence makes clients invent a story. A concise, honest note lets them make decisions while there is still time to help.
