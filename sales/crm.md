# CRM Process

> **Purpose:** Preserve prospect context and prevent relationship management from living in memory.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Growth Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Sales Discovery](discovery.md), [Follow-up and Closing](follow-up-and-closing.md), [Privacy Inputs](../legal/privacy-template.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

Use a simple CRM process from the first inquiry: record source, organisation, contact, business problem, next step, owner, consent or lawful basis for retained information, and outcome. Keep notes factual and respectful; a CRM is not a place for speculation about people.

Review active opportunities weekly. Close records responsibly when a conversation ends and apply an appropriate retention policy after legal review. Choose a CRM tool only when the volume and operational need justify it.
