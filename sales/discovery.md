## Questions to understand

What is changing in the business? Who needs to be served? What is currently difficult? What outcome would make this worthwhile? What has been tried? Who decides? What constraints matter? What happens if nothing changes?

## Qualification

Proceed when there is a meaningful problem, a responsible decision-maker, a feasible path, and enough mutual trust to work openly. Decline or defer when expectations require unsupported promises, hidden scope, or a solution Pravaah cannot responsibly provide.

The point is fit, not conversion at any cost. A clear no protects the brand and the prospect.
