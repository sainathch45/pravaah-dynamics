# Follow-up, Objections, and Closing

> **Purpose:** Keep prospective-client communication useful after the first conversation.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Growth Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Sales Discovery](discovery.md), [Follow-up Template](../templates/follow-up-email.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

Follow up with a useful recap and a clear next step. Treat an objection as information: clarify the concern, test whether the underlying assumption is true, and adjust only when doing so preserves the quality and honesty of the engagement.

Closing means confirming fit, scope, agreement, owners, and kickoff readiness. It does not mean using urgency or ambiguity to force a decision.
