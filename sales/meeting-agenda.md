# Discovery Meeting Agenda

> **Purpose:** Give early conversations enough shape to uncover the real business need.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Growth Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Sales Discovery](discovery.md), [Discovery Notes](../templates/discovery-notes.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 2 minutes

1. Set the purpose and confirm the available time.
2. Understand the business, audience, and current situation.
3. Explore the constraint, prior attempts, intended outcome, and evidence.
4. Clarify stakeholders, constraints, timing, and decision process.
5. Summarise what was heard, name open questions, and agree on the right next step.

The agenda is a guide, not a script. Follow the most useful line of inquiry rather than rushing toward a preselected service.
