# Pricing Framework

> **Purpose:** Guide pricing conversations without pretending every problem has a fixed answer.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Proposals](proposals.md), [Maintenance](../operations/maintenance.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

Price for the value and responsibility of the work, the uncertainty being absorbed, the expertise required, and the sustainable capacity needed to deliver well. Do not invent a universal rate card before experience supports one.

Use the three engagements as a way to frame the problem: Foundations, Experiences, and Systems. Scope and price are shaped after discovery. A proposal should make the trade-offs between depth, speed, risk, and investment visible.
