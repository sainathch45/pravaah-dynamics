# Proposals and Client Expectations

> **Purpose:** Turn a shared understanding into a clear commercial proposal.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Growth Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Proposal Template](../templates/proposal.md), [Pricing Framework](pricing.md), [Legal Templates](../legal/README.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 4 minutes

A proposal explains the business context, intended outcome, recommended approach, scope, exclusions, responsibilities, timing assumptions, investment, and next step. It should make a decision easier, not obscure it with volume.

Never promise outcomes that depend on the client, market, or third parties beyond the agreed work. State dependencies and change control clearly because a good relationship needs an honest boundary.
