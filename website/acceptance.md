# Product 001 Acceptance Criteria

> **Purpose:** Define what must be true before Product 001 is considered ready to launch.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Founders  
> **Related Documents:** [Performance](performance.md), [Accessibility](accessibility.md), [Launch Checklist](../templates/launch-checklist.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 4 minutes

Before launch, verify that:

- The narrative explains Pravaah, its three engagements, and the inquiry next step without unsupported claims.
- The inquiry journey works end to end, has clear validation, and has a named response owner.
- Public pages meet the accessibility baseline and reduced-motion behavior.
- Performance is measured against the stated budget on representative mobile conditions.
- Metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots directives, and verified structured data are present where applicable.
- Images, third-party tools, analytics, error monitoring, privacy, cookies, and legal links have documented approval and operational owners.
- Deployment, domain, rollback, and incident-response details are tested and recorded.

An attractive interface is not sufficient for launch. Readiness means the experience is true, usable, fast, observable, and maintainable.
