# Product 001 Accessibility Requirements

> **Purpose:** Define the public-product accessibility baseline before implementation.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Technology Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Design Accessibility](../design/accessibility.md), [Acceptance Criteria](acceptance.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 4 minutes

Product 001 targets WCAG 2.2 AA. This includes semantic structure, keyboard operation, visible focus, meaningful form labels and errors, correct heading hierarchy, adequate contrast, descriptive alternatives for non-text content, and support for zoom and reduced motion.

Test the inquiry path without a mouse, with a screen reader smoke test, at narrow and wide layouts, and with motion reduced. Accessibility issues block launch when they prevent understanding, navigation, or successful inquiry.
