# Product 001 Analytics

> **Purpose:** Measure whether the site is useful while respecting visitor privacy.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Growth Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Privacy and Cookies](privacy-and-cookies.md), [Performance](performance.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

## Questions worth measuring

- Can visitors reach and submit the conversation form?
- Which entry and engagement paths lead to qualified conversations?
- Are technical issues preventing people from using the site?

Select analytics and error-monitoring providers only at implementation time, after documenting data collection, consent requirements, access owners, retention, and the reason each tool is necessary. Do not collect sensitive form contents in analytics events. Review the measurement plan when the site’s purpose changes.
