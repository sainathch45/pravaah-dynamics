# Product 001 Copywriting Guide

> **Purpose:** Ensure website copy explains real value without agency clichés or unsupported claims.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Growth Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Pravaah Language](../company/language.md), [Information Architecture](information-architecture.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 4 minutes

## Role of copy

The copy should help a business leader name a problem, understand Pravaah’s way of working, and decide whether a conversation is worthwhile. It should not imitate a large agency or make claims that the company cannot evidence.

## Narrative shape

1. Begin with business momentum and the cost of unclear digital experiences.
2. Explain the Pravaah belief: trust is created through strategy, design, and dependable technology.
3. Present Foundations, Experiences, and Systems as ways to solve different constraints.
4. Describe the collaborative approach and what the first conversation is for.
5. Invite a specific, low-pressure next step.

## Rules

- Write in short paragraphs with concrete nouns and verbs.
- Explain an engagement through the business problem it addresses, not a catalogue of deliverables.
- Do not claim results, clients, awards, scale, or history before they are true.
- Avoid prohibited language in [Pravaah Language](../company/language.md).
- Do not describe Pravaah as selling services. Describe the problems it helps solve.
