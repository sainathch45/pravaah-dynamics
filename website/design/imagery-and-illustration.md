## Phase 2 decision

Product 001 launches successfully without photography or illustration if no purposeful, approved asset exists. Typography, spacing, the flow line, and honest copy carry the experience. This is intentional: image quantity does not equal a richer brand.

## Future photography mood

When photography is approved, it should feel observed, composed, and materially honest. Prefer architecture, working environments, tactile details, natural light, paper, stone, metal, shadow, and real moments of concentration. Use modest contrast, restrained colour, and ample negative space. Crops should leave room for content rather than treating every image as a banner.

Never use generic stock team meetings, smiling headset operators, robotic AI imagery, exaggerated device mockups, or images that imply clients, offices, projects, or scale that Pravaah cannot substantiate.

## Illustration direction

Illustration is reserved for explaining a real system, process, or relationship that language cannot make clear enough. It uses a fine-line, geometric, editorial language: ink, paper, and moss only; no gradients, glows, cartoon figures, or dense technical diagrams. Every illustration requires adjacent text that conveys its meaning.

## Texture and graphic language

The flow line is the primary graphic device. A faint paper grain may be used only if it adds less than 20KB compressed, does not reduce contrast, and is static. No decorative noise layer, moving mesh, 3D orb, glass effect, or continuous background animation is permitted.

## Image-slot specification

Any approved image slot declares a purpose, source owner, aspect ratio, focal point, alt text, and responsive crop strategy before implementation. Below-fold images load lazily. The Home v1.0.0 design includes no required image slots; Layer 3 may add approved placements through a design review and ADR if they change the composition materially.
