# Future Pages and Content Governance

> **Purpose:** Prevent the public site from growing into an unstructured catalogue.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Growth Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Information Architecture](information-architecture.md), [Journal Framework](../marketing/journal.md), [Case Study Framework](../marketing/case-studies.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

Add a page only when it serves a distinct visitor question that the existing narrative cannot answer. Candidate future pages include real case studies, approved Journal entries, a team page once it adds useful context, and product pages when those products exist.

Every new page needs an owner, audience, purpose, source of truth, review date, and metadata. Retire or revise pages that become inaccurate. A smaller accurate site is better than a large stale one.
