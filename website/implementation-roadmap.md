## Approval gate

No code begins until founders approve this Handbook, the Product 001 specification, selected legal posture, and implementation scope.

## Phase 1: product and content readiness

Confirm final domain, legal entity path, inquiry recipient and response process, real contact details, page copy, imagery direction, and selected privacy-conscious vendors.

## Phase 2: foundation

Create the approved Next.js project, design tokens, content model, deployment environments, quality gates, and accessible shell. Record implementation decisions that differ from the recommendation.

## Phase 3: narrative and conversion

Build and review the Home, Approach, Engagements, Journal landing state, and conversation flow against the product specification. Test content hierarchy, mobile behavior, accessibility, and performance as each area is added.

## Phase 4: launch readiness

Complete metadata, verified structured data, analytics, error monitoring, legal routes, production configuration, and launch checklist. Perform end-to-end, accessibility, performance, and recovery checks.

## Phase 5: learn and improve

Review inquiry quality, user friction, performance, and operational lessons. Add real Journal or case-study content only through the relevant governance process.
