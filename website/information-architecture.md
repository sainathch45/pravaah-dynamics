## Initial structure

- **Home** — a narrative introduction to the problem Pravaah solves, its point of view, the three engagements, and the invitation to talk.
- **Approach** — how Pravaah thinks, collaborates, and turns ambiguity into progress.
- **Engagements** — Foundations, Experiences, and Systems as problem frames, not rigid service packages.
- **Journal** — an honest editorial landing state explaining the kind of thinking that will be published later.
- **Start a conversation** — the inquiry form and expectation-setting path.
- **Legal routes** — privacy and cookie information only after review and facts are complete.

## Core journeys

### A leader with an unclear digital problem

Arrives at Home → recognizes their challenge in plain language → understands the three ways Pravaah helps → reads Approach for confidence → starts a conversation.

### A referred prospect validating fit

Arrives at Engagements or Approach → understands the working style and truth boundary → supplies concise context → receives a clear next-step expectation.

### A future reader

Arrives at Journal → understands that Pravaah publishes considered work → can return when approved writing exists. No empty article grid or fabricated publication history.

The navigation remains small because the public need is clarity, not a tour of an internal organisation chart.
