# Product 001 Motion Specification

> **Purpose:** Translate the Pravaah motion principles into product behavior.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Technology Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Motion System](../design/motion.md), [Accessibility](accessibility.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

Use motion for page orientation, hover and focus feedback, disclosure, and form state confirmation. Prefer opacity and small positional transitions that preserve the reading rhythm. Avoid scroll spectacle, looping decoration, disruptive page entrances, and motion that changes the meaning of content.

All non-essential motion must reduce or disappear when the user requests reduced motion. Motion specifications are reviewed with design and performance together because a beautiful interaction that is slow or inaccessible contradicts the product goal.
