# Product 001 Performance Budget

> **Purpose:** Protect the calm, fast experience implied by the Pravaah brand.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Technology Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Technology](technology.md), [Engineering Performance](../engineering/performance.md), [Acceptance Criteria](acceptance.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

Performance is part of trust. The site should prioritize readable content and usable inquiry over ornamental media or scripts.

## Budget targets

- Aim for a real-user LCP at or below 2.5 seconds on representative mobile connections.
- Keep CLS at or below 0.1 and INP at or below 200 milliseconds where measured.
- Avoid auto-playing media, large unoptimized imagery, unused client-side JavaScript, and third-party scripts without a documented purpose.
- Use responsive, optimised images and defer non-essential resources.

Measure before launch and after meaningful releases. When a trade-off exceeds the budget, document why and decide deliberately.
