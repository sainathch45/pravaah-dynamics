# Product 001 Privacy and Cookies

> **Purpose:** Define the facts and approvals needed before public tracking or privacy policies exist.
>
> **Pravaah OS:** v1.0.0  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-08  
> **Owner:** Technology Lead  
> **Related Documents:** [Privacy Inputs](../legal/privacy-template.md), [Legal Review](../legal/legal-review.md), [Analytics](analytics.md)  
> **Estimated Reading Time:** 3 minutes

No final privacy policy, cookie policy, or consent mechanism is specified until Pravaah has a legal entity, final contact details, implemented form fields, selected processors, and qualified legal review.

At implementation, collect only the data needed for the inquiry and operations. Document every processor and cookie. Obtain consent where required by applicable law and ensure the product remains usable when non-essential tracking is declined.
