## SEO approach

Search visibility follows useful content, clear structure, performance, and accurate page descriptions. Product 001 will target business-intent language naturally through its engagement and approach pages; it will not create thin location pages or repeat keywords without purpose.

## Metadata

Each public page needs a unique factual title, concise description, canonical URL, social-sharing title and description, and an intentional social image. Metadata must be updated with the actual deployment domain.

## Structured data

After the legal business identity is final, add only verified Organisation and WebSite structured data. Add Article or case-study structured data only to real, published content. Do not publish aggregate ratings, false reviews, or unsupported claims.

## Technical essentials

Generate sitemap and robots directives appropriate to the production domain. Use descriptive heading structure, meaningful links, and image alt text that explains content rather than repeats keywords.
